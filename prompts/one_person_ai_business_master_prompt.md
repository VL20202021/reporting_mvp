Works with ChatGPT (Deep Research), Claude / Claude Code, Gemini (Deep Research), Grok (DeepSearch) and Mistral (Le Chat, web search on).
Paste **Part A** into every model, then add that model's line from **Part B**. Fill in the `[FOUNDER PROFILE]` first; the more you fill in, the less generic the answer.

- Run Part A as a fresh chat in each model. Don't paste the whole thing in a chat with history.
