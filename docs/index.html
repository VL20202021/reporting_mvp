Questions we hear

Before you call

Straight answers. If yours is missing, ask it on the discovery call.

Do you sell robots?

No. We help you choose and we manage the project. You buy, lease or rent from the manufacturer's French channel or an integrator.

Can you guarantee the return on investment?

No one honestly can. We show a low, base and high case using your real labour costs and number of shifts. Labour hours freed become cash savings only if positions are not refilled.

Who is responsible for safety and CE marking?

The integrator or manufacturer who builds the installation. We check that every quote names that responsibility and the risk assessment. We hold professional liability insurance for our advice.

What about our staff and works council?

We plan the works-council (CSE) information and consultation from the start, and a training and redeployment plan. Robots take the painful, repetitive tasks; people move to work that needs judgement.

Are there subsidies?

Sometimes. CRAMIF's ergonomics subsidy can reach €25k per company for owned, new equipment on its list; rented robots are not eligible. Bpifrance and the Region have other schemes. We check eligibility; we never promise approval.

Can we pay monthly?

Yes, through leasing or robot-as-a-service offers. We compare them with buying and check uptime, penalty, liability and exit clauses.
