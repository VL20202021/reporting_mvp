# Research notes: AI-native companies listing — Germany

Scope (agreed 2026-11-16): AI-native only; 1 Jan 2024 – 16 Nov 2026; all stages (listed, BaFin-approved prospectus, intention to float, reported bank mandates / confidential filings); Frankfurt Prime/General Standard + Scale, plus overseas listings by German AI companies. Output: separate PDF.
Note: Germany has no public draft-prospectus stage; BaFin-approved prospectuses are published at launch.

## Stage 1 — candidate discovery (web search; all secondary until checked)
- No AI-native company found listing on Frankfurt (any segment) in Jan 2024 – Nov 2026.
- innoscripta SE: Scale IPO, first trading 23 May 2025, EUR 120/share, secondary-only offer; R&D tax-incentive software (Clusterix) — NOT AI-native -> excluded. [https://www.deutsche-boerse.com/dbg-en/media/news-stories/press-releases/innoscripta-SE-joins-the-Scale-segment-of-the-Frankfurt-Stock-Exchange--4398522]
- DeepL (Cologne, AI translation): Bloomberg (2025) reported early talks with advisers on US listing as early as 2026, ~US$5bn valuation; CEO on CNBC 1 Jun 2026: not seeking IPO now, markets not open for companies its size (also reported 25% layoff). [https://uk.investing.com/news/company-news/deepl-considers-us-ipo-at-potential-5-billion-valuation-93CH-4289721 ; https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/06/01/deepl-ceo-decision-to-lay-off-25-percent-of-workforce-one-of-the-hardest-you-can-make.html]
- Celonis (Munich, process mining/AI): reported US IPO ambition, no filing. [https://www.data-unplugged.de/en/blog/german-ai] — weak source.
- Helsing (Munich, defence AI): valued ~US$18bn after Series E (Jul 2026); no IPO date. [https://www.311institute.com/startups/helsing/] — weak source.
- n8n (Berlin, AI workflow automation): CEO prefers European/German listing, not in a rush; no filing. [https://www.revenuememo.com/p/n8n-funding] — weak source.
- Aleph Alpha: definitive merger agreement with Cohere (Sep 2026); combined company operates as Cohere -> no standalone listing. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleph_Alpha]
- Black Forest Labs, Parloa: no IPO plans found.
- Frankfurt AI-adjacent listings by non-German companies (Cerebras reported in one table) -> out of scope (not German).
