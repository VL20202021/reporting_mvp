# 1. Executive summary

- **No German AI-native company has listed in the period** on any Frankfurt segment (Prime Standard, General Standard or Scale) or overseas. Frankfurt's IPOs in 2024–2026 were dominated by defence, industrials and energy.
- **The one software IPO was not an AI company.** innoscripta SE listed on Scale in May 2025 at €120. Deutsche Börse's own release describes it as R&D tax-incentive software, so it does not count as AI-native.
- **The pipeline consists of statements and press reports, not filings:**
  - **DeepL** discussed a US listing in 2025, but its CEO said in June 2026 it is not seeking an IPO now.
  - **Celonis** is reported to be preparing a US IPO at US$11–13bn. This is a borderline case for "AI-native".
  - **Helsing** (valued at about US$18bn) and **n8n** have no IPO timetable.
- **Germany's best-known foundation-model company will not list on its own.** Aleph Alpha signed a definitive agreement on 16 Sep 2026 to combine with Cohere under the Cohere name.
- **Net effect:** German AI exposure is reachable through public markets today only indirectly. The likely first listings, DeepL or Celonis, point to New York rather than Frankfurt.

**Germany has no public draft-prospectus stage.** BaFin-approved prospectuses are published only at launch, and US confidential filings are not public. So for every pipeline company here, **no primary filing exists to fetch**. This is a structural limit, not an access problem.

# 5. Pipeline notes

**DeepL (Cologne).**

- Bloomberg reported in 2025 that DeepL held early talks with advisers about a US listing as early as 2026, at a possible valuation of about US$5bn.
- On CNBC on 1 June 2026, the CEO said DeepL is not seeking an IPO now and that IPO markets are not open for companies its size. The same coverage reported a 25% workforce reduction.
- A later report (October 2026) says advisers were engaged again. The signals conflict.

**Celonis (Munich).**

- German finance sites report preparation for an H2 2026 listing, likely in the US, at US$11–13bn.
- Forge says the company has not officially endorsed an IPO, and no filing has been found.
- Celonis positions process mining as the data layer for enterprise AI. It is classed here as **borderline** rather than AI-native.

**Helsing (Munich).**

- A defence AI company, reported valued at about US$18bn after a Series E round in July 2026.
- No IPO timetable has been announced.

**n8n (Berlin).**

- Its CEO has said he would prefer a European, ideally German, listing but is "not in a rush". There is no filing.

**Aleph Alpha (Heidelberg).**

- Cohere and Aleph Alpha signed a definitive combination agreement on 16 Sep 2026, following a plan announced in April 2026.
- The combined company will operate as Cohere, headquartered in both Berlin and Toronto. Financial terms were not disclosed; a combined value of about US$20bn was reported.
- This removes the most-cited candidate for a German "sovereign AI" listing.

# 6. Observations

1. **Germany is an absent market for AI listings.** Frankfurt has had a working IPO window, but it was used by defence, industrial and energy issuers.
2. **The likely first German AI IPO will happen abroad.** DeepL and Celonis are both reported to be weighing US listings. Only n8n has expressed a preference for Germany.
3. **Consolidation is a substitute for listing.** Aleph Alpha's combination with Cohere shows the alternative path: sovereign-AI positioning delivered through a merger rather than an IPO.
4. **Expect late visibility.** With no public draft stage in Germany, and confidential filing in the US, the first hard document for any German AI IPO will appear only weeks before trading.
