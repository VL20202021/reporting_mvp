  - Use of funds (s.1.7.1): after full repayment of 2018 convertible bonds (OCA 2018), ~60% to build the organisation (sales & marketing force, engineer hiring, commercial spend), ~40% to technology (new Paradigm features: agents, multimodal RAG; fine-tuning sector models; compute for R&D).
  - Indicative timetable: first trading on Euronext Growth 26 Nov 2024 (line "LIGHTON").
  - Self-description: "LightOn est un acteur européen de premier plan du secteur de l'intelligence artificielle ('IA')".
