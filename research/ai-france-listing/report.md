# 1. Executive summary

- **One French AI-native company has listed in the period: LightOn.** It listed on Euronext Growth Paris on **26 November 2024** (ticker ALTAI) and raised **€11.9m gross** at **€10.35 per share**. Its market capitalisation at the IPO was about **€62m**. All of this is verified from the AMF-approved prospectus and the company's offer-results release.
- **It was small, and it has been hard going since.**
  - Demand covered the offer 1.5x, and retail investors supplied about 65% of the money.
  - Revenue fell from €7.97m in FY2023 to €1.13m in FY2024. It recovered only to €1.74m in FY2025.
  - The net loss widened to €7.4m in FY2025, and cash fell from €12.1m to €3.9m over the year.
  - The share price was reported at about €4.9 in early 2026, roughly half the IPO price (secondary source).
- **There is no other listing and no live public filing.**
  - **Mistral AI**, France's best-funded AI company, has no announced IPO plans.
  - **Dataiku** is French-founded but now headquartered in New York. It reportedly hired banks for a US IPO in October 2025, but no public filing exists.
- **France's AI listing story so far is one micro-cap on the growth market.** Mainboard-scale French AI companies are funding themselves privately.

# 4. Company profile: LightOn SA (Euronext Growth Paris: ALTAI)

**Business.** The company describes itself as *"un acteur européen de premier plan du secteur de l'intelligence artificielle"* (a leading European player in AI; securities note). Its product is **Paradigm**, a generative-AI platform for enterprise search and reasoning that is sold as a licence. The offer-results release calls it the "first generative AI IPO in Europe", a company claim citing Dealroom.

**Offer terms:**

| Item | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| AMF approvals | Registration document I.24-012 (21 Oct 2024); supplement I.24-015 and prospectus 24-475 (both 7 Nov 2024) | Securities note, cover |
| Offer type | Fixed-price public offer in France plus a global placement with institutions | Securities note §4.1 |
| Price | €10.35 per share, fixed by the board on 6 Nov 2024 | Securities note §4.1 |
| Shares issued | 1,150,000 new shares, after the 15% extension clause was exercised | Offer-results release, 21 Nov 2024 |
| Gross proceeds | €11.9m (up to €13.5m with the over-allotment option) | Offer-results release |
| Demand | €15.2m (1,467,052 shares), i.e. 1.5x. Retail €8.9m; 6,379 individual investors took 65% of the offer | Offer-results release |
| Market cap at IPO | ≈ €62m; free float 19.1% | Offer-results release |
| First trading | 26 Nov 2024, ISIN FR0013230950 | Offer-results release; securities note timetable |
| Lead | Portzamparc (BNP Paribas group), global coordinator | Securities note |

**Use of proceeds** (securities note §1.7.1). Estimated net proceeds were €8.7m for a 100% offer and €10.2m with the extension clause. The 2018 convertible bonds (OCA 2018) are repaid in full first. The remainder is split:

| Use | Share of net proceeds |
|---|---|
| Building the organisation: sales and marketing force, hiring engineers, commercial spend | ~60% |
| Technology: new Paradigm features (agents, multimodal RAG), fine-tuning sector-specific models, compute for R&D | ~40% |

The company attributes slower ARR growth to contract signatures slipping. Its stated reasons are clients waiting for GPUs and long tender or pilot cycles.

**Read-across:**

- **FY2023 was an outlier year.** FY2023 revenue was 4x FY2022, followed by an 86% drop in FY2024. The registration document does not attribute this to a single contract in what I extracted. My inference is a shift from one-off contracts to Paradigm licensing, but that is not confirmed from the filings.
- **Cash is tight.** By end-2025 the IPO cash had largely been spent. Secondary reports say LightOn raised a €3.7m convertible bond in April 2026 and announced up to €4m of further bond financing in September 2026, which extends its cash runway to September 2027.

# 5. Observations

1. **The French AI listing market is one micro-cap deep.** LightOn's €11.9m raise is a micro-cap deal on the growth market, and it relied heavily on retail demand.
2. **Results since listing illustrate the risk.** Revenue has not regained its FY2023 level, losses have grown, and the company has turned to convertible debt. That record will shape how investors price the next French AI IPO.
3. **The scaled French AI companies are not coming to Paris:**
   - Mistral AI is raising large private rounds.
   - Dataiku, French-founded, is lining up a US listing.
   - The pattern points to the US, or to staying private longer, rather than Euronext.
4. **There is little early visibility.** With no draft-prospectus stage, the next French AI IPO will become visible only weeks before pricing, when the AMF approves the registration document.

# 6. Limitations

- Euronext's own press-release page could not be parsed. The offer-results release (DILA/AMF) and the securities note were used instead.
- The DILA file for LightOn's H1 2026 release could not be located, so those figures are secondary.
- US filings (SEC EDGAR) could not be read from this environment. Dataiku has no public filing in any case.
- There is no public list of AMF registration-document approvals filtered by sector, so candidate discovery relied on search. A quiet Euronext Growth AI listing could have been missed.
