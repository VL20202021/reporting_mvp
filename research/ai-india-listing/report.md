# 1. Executive summary

- **One AI-native company has listed in India in the period: Fractal Analytics.** It listed on NSE and BSE on **16 February 2026** after a **₹2,833.9 crore** IPO at **₹900 per share** (top of a ₹857–900 band). This is verified from the prospectus and the company's FY2025-26 annual report.
- **The IPO shrank by about 42% between filing and launch.** Coverage of the August 2025 DRHP put the issue at about ₹4,900 crore. The final prospectus shows ₹2,833.9 crore: a ₹1,023.5 crore fresh issue plus a ₹1,810.4 crore offer for sale by Apax (Quinag Bidco), TPG, GLM Family Trust and one individual holder.
- **Little of the fresh money goes to "AI R&D", contrary to the headlines.** Only ₹355.1 crore (37% of ₹959.3 crore net proceeds) is earmarked for R&D and sales & marketing in the Fractal Alpha segment. The largest single line is ₹264.9 crore to repay borrowings at Fractal USA.
- **Fractal has grown and stayed profitable since listing.** FY26 revenue was ₹3,300 crore (+19%) and net income ₹287 crore (+30%). In Q2 FY27 revenue reached ₹956.5 crore (+20% YoY) and net income ₹79.2 crore (+139%). All of these figures come from the company's exchange filings.
- **The rest of the pipeline is thin and mostly unverifiable:**
  - **C5i (formerly Course5 Intelligence)** has reportedly filed by SEBI's confidential route, targeting ₹1,000–1,200 crore. Confidential filings are not public.
  - **Meganet Technologies Global** filed a DRHP today (16 Nov 2026) for about ₹740 crore. It is AI-infrastructure hardware, a borderline case for "AI-native".
  - **Yotta Data Services** (GPU cloud) tried a Nasdaq listing through a SPAC merger. The merger was terminated in January 2026, and Yotta is now reported to be preparing an Indian IPO.
- **No India-based AI-native company completed an overseas listing in the window.**

- **Fractal.ai:** AI services, plus AI products hosted mainly on its Cogentiq platform.

## 4.2 C5i (formerly Course5 Intelligence) — Confidential pre-filing

- **Filing:** reported to have filed a DRHP with SEBI by the confidential pre-filing route around July 2026, targeting about ₹1,000–1,200 crore.
- **History:** this would be its second attempt. Course5 Intelligence filed a ₹600 crore DRHP in January 2022, before this report's window, and later shelved it.
- **Disclosure:** under SEBI's confidential route the document is not public until an updated DRHP (UDRHP-I) is filed. No figures could be verified.

Sources: Inc42, Venture Intelligence (Section 8).

## 4.3 Yotta Data Services (via Nidar Infrastructure Ltd) — Overseas attempt, now India pipeline

**Nasdaq de-SPAC attempt:**

- Nidar Infrastructure, Yotta's holding company, agreed in June 2024 to merge with Cartica Acquisition Corp, aiming for a Nasdaq listing under the ticker "YTTA".
- The Form F-4 was reported effective in November 2025.
- Cartica shareholders approved the deal on 4 Dec 2025.
- The parties reportedly terminated the merger on 7 Jan 2026, and Cartica moved to liquidate.

**Indian IPO plans:**

- Subsequent reports say Yotta plans an Indian IPO of up to US$1.5 bn.
- A DRHP was targeted for around October 2026, but no public DRHP was found as of 16 Nov 2026.
- Yotta's CEO is quoted saying about 50% of revenue comes from GPUs.

**Verification:** none of this could be checked against the SEC filings, because EDGAR returned HTTP 403 to automated requests.

## 4.4 Meganet Technologies Global — DRHP filed 16 Nov 2026

- **Filing:** Reuters reports a DRHP for about ₹740 crore: up to ₹140 crore fresh issue and ₹600 crore OFS.
- **Business:** builds servers for high-performance computing, data centres and AI workloads.
- **Customers and partners:** customers include Yotta and Motilal Oswal; it has sourcing tie-ups with Nvidia and AMD.
- **Verification:** the DRHP was not yet on SEBI's draft-offer list when checked, so nothing is verified.
- **Classification:** included as borderline because it is a hardware integrator rather than an AI-model or AI-software company.

# 5. Observations

1. **India's "AI IPO" market is so far one deal deep.** Fractal is the only AI-native listing in nearly three years. Many companies call themselves AI firms in their filings (security, SaaS, telecom), but under a strict AI-native test they fall away.
2. **Primary documents contradict two widely repeated headlines:**
   - The IPO was described as "₹4,900 crore"; the final issue was ₹2,833.9 crore.
   - The proceeds were described as going to "AI/GenAI R&D"; the largest single use is US debt repayment, and R&D plus S&M is 37% of the net proceeds.
3. **The pipeline is moving toward AI infrastructure.** The next candidates are mostly compute and hardware (Yotta, Meganet), not model or application companies. Well-funded model and app startups (Sarvam AI, Qure.ai, Gupshup) have not filed.
4. **Overseas listing has not worked so far.** The one India-based AI attempt in the window, Yotta's Nasdaq SPAC, was terminated, and Yotta has turned to the domestic market.
5. **Disclosure gaps will persist.** Confidential pre-filing (C5i) means investors and analysts see nothing until the UDRHP. Expect late visibility on the next AI issues.

# 6. Limitations

- Only Fractal's data was verified end to end.
- SEC EDGAR (HTTP 403, needs a declared contact User-Agent) and NSE archive PDFs (connection refused) could not be read.
- SEBI's firewall blocked paginated searches of draft offer documents, so the candidate list depends partly on news discovery and may miss quiet filers.
- "AI-native" is a judgement call. Borderline cases are flagged rather than silently included.
