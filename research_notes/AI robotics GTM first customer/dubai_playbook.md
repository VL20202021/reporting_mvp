# Dubai / UAE Go-to-Market Playbook: AI Software + Robotics Hardware, First Paying Customer

Research date: 2026-11-07. Labels: **[Fact]** = sourced fact; **[Opinion]** = expert/commentator view or promotional claim; **[Inference]** = researcher's reasoning, not directly sourced. Research budget was limited (~16 searches, no full-page fetches), so most citations rest on search-result summaries of the linked pages. Re-check figures before committing money.

## 1. Government-led demand (Dubai): DFF, DFA, Robotics Program, AI Blueprint, RTA, DEWA, Police, Municipality

### Takeaway
Dubai has an explicit, top-down robotics mandate: the Dubai Robotics and Automation Program, launched by Sheikh Hamdan in September 2022 and run by the Dubai Future Foundation (DFF), aims for robotics to reach 9% of GDP and 200,000 robots within 10 years. The realistic way in for a startup is an entity-sponsored pilot through DFF channels (DFA, Dubai Future Labs, DCAI accelerators). These channels have historically produced MoUs and pilots, not guaranteed procurement contracts.

### Cited Findings
**Dubai Robotics and Automation (R&A) Program**
- [Fact] Sheikh Hamdan launched it in September 2022. It is led by DFF and targets robotics and automation at 9% of Dubai's GDP within 10 years. The official UAE portal says "before 2032"; a 2026 Pulse article says 2034 (a discrepancy). — [u.ae portal](https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/strategies-initiatives-and-awards/strategies-plans-and-visions/industry-science-and-technology/dubai-robotics-and-automation-program); [DFF announcement](https://www.dubaifuture.ae/dubai-future-foundation/hamdan-bin-mohammed-launches-the-dubai-robotics-and-automation-program); [WAM](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/hszrfj2a-hamdan-bin-mohammed-launches-dubai-robotics-and); [Pulse (2026)](https://www.pulse.bot/robotics/news/the-desert-digitalises-how-dubai-is-engineering-a-global-hub-for-robotics-and-automation-15068c3f-d4dc-4fc3-9996-3fa7598146cb/)

### Inferences
- [Inference] The highest-probability first paying customer path is: DFF/DFA or a DCAI accelerator, then an entity-sponsored pilot (with a paid pilot fee where possible), then entity procurement via eSupply. Expect the MoU stage to be unpaid unless negotiated otherwise.
- [Inference] Best-fit entity buyers for AI plus robotics are RTA (last-mile and autonomous zones), DEWA (inspection robots, drones), Dubai Police (patrol and security), Dubai Municipality (inspection, cleaning, drones) and Expo City Dubai (a test-bed district).
- [Inference] Micropolis is the incumbent local champion for Dubai Police UGVs. A foreign startup should position as complementary (software, specialised manipulators) or target other entities.

## 3. Free zones, company setup, costs, Golden Visa

### Takeaway
The cheapest credible Dubai entry for an AI company is the DIFC AI Licence (Dubai AI Campus), at about US$1,500 (~AED 5,500) a year with coworking and discounted visas. DMCC (Dubai AI Centre) costs roughly AED 35–50k in year 1. Mainland versus free zone matters for direct selling onshore. This pass did not verify which entities require a mainland licence on eSupply.

### Cited Findings
- [Fact] DIFC AI Licence: subsidised (about 90%) at US$1,500 a year, with coworking access, discounted visas and multiple activities at no extra cost. Located in Dubai AI Campus (Innovation One). Campus targets by 2028: more than US$300m raised by members, 500+ AI startups, 3,000+ jobs. — [DIFC AI licence page](https://difc-experienceedge-prod-57prnepj5.vercel.app/business/establish-a-business/ai-licence); [Dubai AI Campus setup guide](https://www.dubaiaicampus.com/files/Dubai%20AI%20License%20Setup%20Guide.pdf); [The National 2023](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/technology/2023/08/14/dubai-to-issue-commercial-licences-to-help-ai-and-web3-companies-set-up-businesses)
- [Fact] DMCC official: registration from AED 9,000 (one-off), licence AED 10,000–50,000 a year by activity type. — [DMCC blog](https://dmcc.ae/blog/dubai-freezone-company-setup-cost)
- [Opinion] Third-party 2026 estimates for DMCC: licence about AED 20,200, plus mandatory flexi-desk AED 15,050, plus registration AED 10,010. Year 1 is roughly AED 35–50k (US$9.5–13.6k), and up to AED 46,780 with one visa per another consultancy. These figures are inconsistent. — [arxsetup](https://arxsetup.pages.dev/guides/dmcc-company-setup-cost); [avyanco](https://avyanco.com/dmcc-free-zone-cost/)
- [Fact] Free zone companies can distribute in the UAE through registered commercial agents without a mainland company. — [Mondaq 2022 law](https://www.mondaq.com/contracts-and-commercial-law/1408916/navigating-the-landscape-understanding-the-commercial-agency-law-of-2022-of-uae)

### Inferences
- [Inference] For a hardware company, DIFC suits the AI and software entity. Physical robot import, storage and assembly likely needs a zone with warehousing or industrial licences (Dubai Silicon Oasis/Dtec, Dubai Science Park, JAFZA). A two-entity structure is common.
- [Inference] Holding a free zone licence that is registered on Dubai eSupply is usually sufficient for Dubai government entities, but some entities require a mainland licence or local partner. Verify per entity.

### Gaps
- No verified costs for in5, Dtec, Dubai Internet City or Dubai Science Park.
- Golden Visa eligibility for founders and innovators not researched.
- No setup timelines verified (consultancy claims of 1–3 weeks are unverified).

## 4. Government procurement, vendor registration, agency law, Emiratisation

### Takeaway
Dubai government tenders run through the eSupply portal (operated by Jaggaer for Dubai Digital Authority). Dubai Law No. 16 of 2016 reserves 10% of procurement by government entities (and entities at least 25% government-owned) for Dubai SME members. That benefit mostly favours Emirati-owned SMEs, so foreign startups compete on the open portion or partner with a local SME.

### Cited Findings
- [Fact] eSupply is the official procurement portal for Dubai government tenders. — [Jaggaer eSupply](https://www.jaggaer.com/esupply); [SME10x](https://www.sme10x.com/technology/jaggaer-launch-revamped-esupply-platform-for-sme-collaboration-with-dubai-government-entities)
- [Fact] Dubai Law No. 16/2016: government entities and those at least 25% government-owned must allocate 10% of procurement to Dubai SME members. Emirati national members are exempt from eSupply registration fees. Federal Law No. 2/2014 likewise sets aside 10% of federal contracts for SMEs. — [Dubai SME Gov Procurement Program](https://sme.ae/service_details/en/membership_in_the_government_procurement_program); [Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/economy/gcc/dubai-sme-enables-emirati-supplier-programme-members-to-secure-4846mln-in-contracts-in-2025-e6ye8qb3)
- [Fact] Emirati Supplier Programme members won AED 1.78bn (~US$485m) of Dubai government contracts in 2025, up 38% from AED 1.29bn in 2024. — [WAM](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/c11agbb-dubai-sme-enables-emirati-supplier-programme)
- [Fact] Federal level: MOHRE lists a 10% evaluation-score bonus for SMEs. MOF federal supplier register requires a trade licence, VAT certificate and owner passport. — [MOHRE procurement](https://mohre.gov.ae/en/procurement); [MOF supplier register](https://mof.gov.ae/supplier-registration-in-federal-supplier-register/)
- [Opinion] A consultancy claims eSupply registration can take 72 hours and that a Tax Clearance Certificate is mandatory by 2026. Unverified. — [HiDubai Focus](https://focus.hidubai.com/how-to-get-on-the-approved-vendor-list-for-2026-government-contracts)
- [Fact] Commercial Agencies Law (Federal Law No. 3/2022) took effect 15–16 June 2023, replacing the 1981 law. Agencies must be registered to be valid. Foreign principals can sell via agents without registering as an agent. Parties can choose arbitration. Registration may be mandatory for supplying certain goods or services to government. — [Tamimi](https://www.tamimi.com/news/a-new-commercial-agencies-law-enacted-in-the-uae/); [Rödl](https://www.roedl.com/insights/united-arab-emirates-commercial-agencies-law-economy-trade); [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/contracts-and-commercial-law/1691290/key-features-of-the-new-uae-commercial-agencies-law)

### Inferences
- [Inference] Practical route: register the UAE entity on eSupply, and on entity-specific vendor portals (e.g. DEWA and RTA run their own supplier registration). Consider teaming with an Emirati SME member to access the 10% set-aside. Avoid exclusive agency agreements early, since the 2022 law still protects registered agents.

### Gaps
- Not verified: entity-specific vendor portals (DEWA, RTA, Dubai Police) and the in-country value (ICV) program, which is used by ADNOC and federal and Abu Dhabi entities and increasingly in Dubai.
- Emiratisation quotas: the 2% annual increase applies to mainland firms with 50+ staff, plus 20–49-staff firms in specified sectors. Not sourced in this pass, so treat as unverified.

## 8. Data, privacy, AI and robotics regulation, payment norms

### Takeaway
The federal PDPL (Decree-Law 45/2021) applies onshore. Its executive regulations were reportedly still unpublished as of May 2026, though sources conflict. DIFC has the most specific AI rule, Regulation 10 on autonomous systems. Payment terms are nominally 30 days for Dubai government suppliers that are SMEs, but delays are common.

### Cited Findings
- [Fact, sources conflict] PDPL executive regulations were not published as a discrete instrument in the Official Gazette as of May 2026 and have been repeatedly delayed. One guide claims they were issued in 2026. — [ATB Legal](https://atblegal.com/regulatory-and-compliance/uae-data-protection/); contradicted by [PromptQuorum](https://www.promptquorum.com/local-llms/uae-pdpl-data-sovereignty-local-ai-2026)
- [Fact] DIFC Regulation 10 (in force since 1 Sept 2023) covers personal data processed by autonomous and semi-autonomous systems. It requires notification to the Commissioner and sets fairness, transparency, security and accountability principles. DIFC Law No. 1/2025 amended the DP law (effective 15 July 2025) and added a private right of action. — [Mondaq](https://webiis08.mondaq.com/data-protection/1734020/ai-regulation-in-the-difc-personal-data-processed-through-autonomous-and-semi-autonomous-systems); [CMS](https://cms.law/en/are/legal-updates/navigating-ai-in-the-uae-a-financial-services-perspective)
- [Fact] ADGM has no AI-specific equivalent of Regulation 10. — [CMS](https://cms.law/en/are/legal-updates/navigating-ai-in-the-uae-a-financial-services-perspective)
- [Fact] Dubai committed to paying SMEs within 30 days instead of 90. A 2021 Dubai financial-regulations resolution allows up to 90 working days from final handover unless the contract says otherwise. Abu Dhabi Circular 1/2019 requires government and state-owned entities to pay within 30 days of invoice, with no clear remedy for late payment. — [Mynewsdesk](https://www.mynewsdesk.com/sg/riabu/news/dubai-government-commits-to-shortening-payment-time-to-smes-364395); [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/economy/sheikh-hamdan-issues-resolution-on-financial-regulations-in-emirate-1.1180293); [Simmons & Simmons](https://www.simmons-simmons.com/publications/ck0a5dcgsmzmz0b858lpov77w/130519-abu-dhabi-government-orders-payment-to-contractors-within-30-days); [BCLP](https://www.bclplaw.com/en-US/insights/is-the-tide-is-turning-for-contractors-and-suppliers-in-the-uae.html)
- [Opinion] Euler Hermes: UAE terms have long been 30 days but increasingly stretch to 60+, especially among large construction and oil and gas firms. — [BCLP](https://www.bclplaw.com/en-US/insights/is-the-tide-is-turning-for-contractors-and-suppliers-in-the-uae.html)

### Inferences
- [Inference] Plan for 60–120 days of cash conversion on government and semi-government deals. Negotiate milestone payments and advance payments against bank guarantees.
- [Inference] Robots with cameras deployed in public spaces will trigger PDPL and Dubai-entity data-residency expectations. Local hosting (UAE cloud regions) helps.

### Gaps
- Not researched: drone regulation (GCAA, plus Dubai Civil Aviation Authority's drone law and registration); robot-specific Dubai law under the R&A program; Dubai AI Seal or AI licensing for government vendors; TDRA/ICT certification for importing radio-equipped robots.

## 9. Pricing and deal-size norms (AED and USD)

### Takeaway
