## 1. Large corporates (CAC 40 / SBF 120) as first customers: how they buy, POC sizes, "POC purgatory"

### Takeaway
French large groups have made public purchasing commitments to startups, and the clearest example in AI robotics is Renault's equity-plus-deployment deal with Wandercraft. Even so, the documented bottleneck is getting from POC to deployment, alongside cybersecurity requirements. The best route is to treat the corporate as a strategic partner (equity or co-development) from day one, rather than to run a standalone POC.

### Cited Findings
- **[Fact]** The EY barometer on the French innovation ecosystem (2026 edition) reports that large groups name two main brakes on the buyer/supplier relationship with startups: the difficulty of moving from POC to deployment, and meeting cybersecurity requirements. — [EY, Performance de l'écosystème d'innovation français](https://www.ey.com/fr_fr/insights/fast-growing-companies/performance-de-l-ecosysteme-d-innovation-francais) (seen as a search summary; the full page fetch was blocked)
- **[Fact]** In 2026, France Digitale and EY added a large-corporate section to their barometer for the first time. It asks about the POC-to-contract conversion rate and whether a simplified purchasing process exists for startups. This indicates that POC conversion is now a tracked ecosystem metric. — [France Digitale/EY questionnaire](https://tally.so/r/1A6VQb)
- **[Fact]** The Médiateur des entreprises published its 2025 report of the "observatoire des relations entre startups et grands comptes" on 2026-02-05. It describes the report as "un appel à l'action", meaning more than a simple state-of-play. The full figures were not retrieved. — [economie.gouv.fr / Médiateur des entreprises](https://www.economie.gouv.fr/node/3239043)
- **[Opinion]** Pierre Pelouzet (then Médiateur des entreprises) said that startups and large accounts "ne travaillent pas assez" (do not work together enough). — [Le Journal des Entreprises](https://www.lejournaldesentreprises.com/article/pierre-pelouzet-mediateur-des-entreprises-start-up-et-grands-comptes-ne-travaillent-pas-assez-2098399)
- **[Fact]** Sopra Steria's Open Innovation Report 2025 finds that 48% of French companies struggle to find the right startups. France was the only European country surveyed to rank this step as the hardest. — (search summary; [Solutions Numériques](https://www.solutions-numeriques.com/?p=237761))
- **[Fact]** A 2019 barometer by Village by CA and Capgemini already reported persistent friction points: payment delays, and the time from contract signature to POC execution. — [LSA](https://www.lsa-conso.fr/les-relations-start-ups-grands-groupes-en-phase-d-industrialisation,317916)
- **[Fact] Renault and Wandercraft (2025):** Renault took a minority stake in Wandercraft and signed an agreement to develop the "Calvin" robot family, first for Renault's own production. — [GlobeNewswire release](https://ml-eu.globenewswire.com/Resource/Download/70500725-e25d-4db7-8610-346dc8af1805); [FrenchWeb](https://www.frenchweb.fr/renault-monte-au-capital-de-wandercraft-et-mise-sur-la-robotique-medicale-pour-reinventer-ses-usines/455216)
  - Calvin was deployed at Renault's Douai plant for tyre handling, with an engineer still supervising operations during this phase. — [Génération NT](https://www.generation-nt.com/actualites/renault-robots-calvin-usines-2077073)
  - Reports cite 350 robots planned for Renault. — [Intelligent CIO](https://www.intelligentcio.com/north-america/?p=54570)
  - Renault might also manufacture robots for Wandercraft in its French plants. — [Journal de l'Automobile](https://journalauto.com/constructeurs/avec-calvin-renault-ouvre-ses-usines-aux-robots-pilotes-par-lia/)
  - Wandercraft raised USD 75M (Series D) in June 2025. — [Maddyness](https://www.maddyness.com/2025/06/11/exosquelettes-wandercraft-leve-75-millions-de-dollars-pour-franchir-un-nouveau-cap/); [Fierce Biotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/medtech/exosuit-maker-wandercraft-raises-75m-alongside-renault-partnership)
- **[Fact]** 14 large groups took on part of the EUR 1bn "Je choisis la French Tech" purchasing commitment announced in June 2025 (see section 2). — [Siècle Digital](https://siecledigital.fr/2025/06/16/je-choisis-la-french-tech-le-programme-franchit-le-cap-du-milliard-deuros-et-vise-leurope/); [La French Tech press kit June 2025](https://lafrenchtech.gouv.fr/app/uploads/2025/06/20250611_DP_JeChoisisLaFrenchTech-1.pdf)
- **[Fact]** Fnac Darty issued a June 2026 VivaTech press release about joining or acting in "Je choisis la French Tech". This shows retailers also act as signatory buyers. — [Fnac Darty CP 17/06/2026](https://www.fnacdarty.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/cp-170626-cpvivatech-jclft-1.pdf)
- **[Fact]** Bpifrance runs a support programme that works with large groups' purchasing departments to favour startups. — [Bpifrance](https://www.bpifrance.com/?p=4185)

### Inferences
- **[Inference]** Wandercraft shows a pattern that is replicable for AI+robotics: (1) a strategic corporate takes a minority stake; (2) a co-designed product variant is built for the corporate's own factory; (3) a narrow first task (tyre handling) runs under human supervision; (4) a volume commitment follows. Pitching a "co-development + volume option" beats pitching a free POC.
- **[Inference]** Because the documented brakes are "POC to deployment" and cybersecurity, a first-customer proposal to a French group should arrive with three things in hand. First, a pre-agreed success-criteria sheet with a conditional purchase order or framework contract triggered on success. Second, a business-unit budget owner, not only the innovation lab. Third, a cybersecurity/IT security pack: data flows, hosting in the EU, and ISO 27001 roadmap or SecNumCloud-hosted options if relevant.
- **[Inference]** Open-innovation units (Airbus BizLab, Saint-Gobain NOVA, L'Oréal, LVMH La Maison des Startups at Station F, Schneider, Engie, TotalEnergies, Michelin, Air Liquide, Orange, Stellantis Ventures, Safran, Thales, Carrefour) work best as door-openers to plant or operations managers. On their own they do not generate revenue.

### Gaps
- No verified 2023-2026 source was retrieved for typical French corporate POC sizes in EUR, or for POC-to-contract conversion percentages. The EY and Médiateur reports likely contain them, but full text was not accessible. Do not quote specific POC price bands without checking these.
- Program-specific details for each named corporate (Airbus BizLab cohort terms, Saint-Gobain NOVA, LVMH La Maison des Startups, Schneider, Engie, etc.) were not verified in this session.
- The exact list of the 14 groups in the JCLFT EUR 1bn commitment was not retrieved.

## 2. Public and semi-public buyers, the innovation procurement threshold, Pacte PME, "Je choisis la French Tech"

### Takeaway
The "achat innovant" exemption still sits at **EUR 100,000 excl. VAT**. Under it, a public buyer can award a contract without advertising or competition. A December 2025 draft decree proposed raising it to EUR 140,000, but the final decree of 29 Dec 2025 did not include that change, so it remains to be verified. "Je choisis la French Tech" passed EUR 2bn of purchasing commitments by June 2026 and is being extended EU-wide.

### Cited Findings
- **[Fact]** Article R.2122-9-1 of the Code de la commande publique allows a contract without prior advertising or competition for innovative works, supplies or services with an estimated value **below EUR 100,000 excl. VAT**. — [collectivites-locales.gouv.fr seuils page](https://www.collectivites-locales.gouv.fr/index.php/animer-les-territoires/commande-publique/les-seuils-et-taux-applicables-aux-contrats-de-la-commande-publique); [Acteurs Publics "100 000 euros"](https://acteurspublics.fr/articles/100-000-euros/)
- **[Fact]** A late-2025 DAJ consultation on a simplification decree proposed aligning the innovative-purchase threshold with the EU supplies/services threshold, which would raise it to **EUR 140,000 excl. VAT**. — [Weka](https://www.weka.fr/actualite/marches-specifiques/article/vers-un-relevement-du-seuil-de-dispense-de-procedure-pour-les-achats-innovants-68607/); [Landot avocats blog](https://blog.landot-avocats.net/2025/11/12/la-daj-lance-une-nouvelle-consultation-concernant-un-decret-a-venir-portant-sur-la-simplification-du-droit-de-la-commande-publique/amp/); [vie-publique consultation synthesis Nov 2025](https://www.vie-publique.fr/files/consultation_publique/PDF/Synth%C3%A8se-consultations-nov2025.pdf)
- **[Fact]** Décret n° 2025-1386 of 29 Dec 2025 made two threshold changes. It raised the general no-advertising/no-competition threshold for supplies and services from EUR 40k to **EUR 60k excl. VAT**. It also made permanent the **EUR 100k** threshold for works. According to Acteurs Publics, it remains unclear whether the innovative-purchase increase was dropped or postponed to a later decree. — [AP-HP Affaires juridiques](https://affairesjuridiques.aphp.fr/textes/decret-n-2025-1386-du-29-decembre-2025-modifiant-certains-seuils-relatifs-aux-marches-publics/); [Acteurs Publics](https://acteurspublics.fr/articles/seuils-accessibilite-le-code-de-la-commande-publique-evolue-dans-une-logique-de-simplification-lexecutif-officialise-diverses-mesures-de-simplification-du-code-de-la-commande-publique/)
- **[Fact]** Bercy proposed further public-procurement simplification measures to apply from 2026. — [Acteurs Publics](https://acteurspublics.fr/articles/de-nouvelles-mesures-proposees-par-bercy-pour-simplifier-la-commande-publique-commande-publique-bercy-propose-de-nouvelles-mesures-pour-simplifier-les-procedures-des-2026/)
- **[Fact]** Bercy published a 2024 guide on public purchasing of innovative solutions. — [FNTP](https://www.fntp.fr/?p=18914)
- **[Fact] "Je choisis la French Tech" (JCLFT):**
  - Launched in 2023 with the aim of doubling public and large-corporate purchases from startups by 2027. An initial reference cited growth from EUR 6bn to EUR 12bn. — [Siècle Digital 2023](https://siecledigital.fr/2023/06/19/le-gouvernement-veut-rapprocher-les-start-up-francaises-et-lindustrie/); [Maddyness 2024 one-year review](https://www.maddyness.com/2024/05/23/je-choisis-la-french-tech-quel-bilan-un-an-apres-son-lancement/)
  - June 2025: **EUR 1bn** of purchase commitments, **12,000** startup-buyer introductions, **730+** signatory companies and **90+** institutional partners. A free online "JCLFT Académie" training on answering public tenders was also launched. — [Siècle Digital](https://siecledigital.fr/2025/06/16/je-choisis-la-french-tech-le-programme-franchit-le-cap-du-milliard-deuros-et-vise-leurope/); [Press kit](https://lafrenchtech.gouv.fr/app/uploads/2025/06/20250611_DP_JeChoisisLaFrenchTech-1.pdf)
  - **17 June 2026:** passed **EUR 2bn** and extended to Europe ("Je choisis la tech européenne"). — [La French Tech CP 805](https://lafrenchtech.gouv.fr/app/uploads/2026/06/805_-_CP_-_Je_choisis_la_French_Tech_franchit_le_cap_des_2_milliards_deuros_et_setend_a_toute_lEurope.pdf) (seen via search summary; direct fetch blocked)
  - Regional committees exist, for example in Bourgogne-Franche-Comté. — [Préfecture BFC](https://www.prefectures-regions.gouv.fr/bourgogne-franche-comte/Actualites/La-prefecture-accueille-le-comite-regional-Je-Choisis-la-French-Tech)
  - **[Opinion]** Critical take: the French Tech "doit faire attention à ce qu'elle choisit" (should be careful about what it chooses). — [Next.ink](https://next.ink/223104/je-choisis-la-french-tech-mais-la-french-tech-doit-faire-attention-a-ce-quelle-choisit/)
  - No independent evaluation of the actual revenue impact on startups was found.
- **[Fact] Pacte PME** (association of large accounts committed to SME relations): its January 2025 barometer reports that a growing number of large accounts pay on time more than 80% of the time, and that late payments of 61-90 days have become residual (3%). — [Pacte PME CP 22 Jan 2025](https://pactepme.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/CP-PactePME-Les-grands-comptes-francais-amplifient-leurs-efforts-pour-soutenir-la-resilience-des-PME-grace-a-des-pratiques-de-paiement-responsables-22-janvier-2025.pdf)

### Inferences
- **[Inference]** For a first public customer, a pilot priced **at or below EUR 100k excl. VAT** and documented as "innovative" can be awarded directly, with no tender. Possible buyers include an SNCF or RATP depot, an AP-HP hospital logistics unit, Groupe ADP or La Poste. Some SNCF/RATP/La Poste/EDF entities are "entités adjudicatrices" under different rules, so check each buyer's status. A lighter route is also available for small deals: anything under the new EUR 60k supplies/services threshold, even if it is not "innovative".
- **[Inference]** Use the JCLFT signatory list as a target list. Signatories have publicly committed purchase volumes and named startup contacts, which shortens cold outreach.
- **[Inference]** UGAP (the central public purchasing body) can list innovative solutions so that public buyers purchase without their own tender. It is worth pursuing after one or two references.

### Gaps
- Not confirmed: whether any 2026 decree raised the innovative threshold to EUR 140k. Check R.2122-9-1 on Légifrance before quoting.
- UGAP's innovation-referencing process and the startup-specific programmes of SNCF, RATP, La Poste, AP-HP, ADP and EDF were not verified this session.
- Current JCLFT 2027 target figures (the EUR 6bn to 12bn baseline) come from 2023 coverage. The latest official target wording was not read in full.

## 3. Funding: Bpifrance, France 2030, tax credits, JEI, EU (EIC)

### Takeaway
Non-dilutive funding can be stacked as follows: Bourse French Tech (up to EUR 30k), BFT Émergence (up to EUR 90k), i-Lab, i-Nov, France 2030 robotics/AI calls, CIR/CII, JEI, and the EIC Accelerator (up to EUR 2.5M grant plus EUR 1-10M equity). The 2025 budget cut the CII rate to 20% and tightened the CIR and JEI criteria.

### Cited Findings
- **[Fact] Bourse French Tech (BFT):** up to **EUR 30,000**, limited to **70%** of eligible expenses, and it cannot exceed the company's equity (fonds propres). — [Hayot Expertise 2026](https://hayot-expertise.fr/blog/bourse-french-tech-bpifrance-conditions); [Legalstart](https://www.legalstart.fr/fiches-pratiques/aides-creation-entreprise/bourse-french-tech/); [Créer Entreprise](https://www.creerentreprise.fr/bourse-french-tech-conditions-aide/)
- **[Fact] BFT Émergence** (deeptech variant): 70% of planned eligible expenses, up to **EUR 90,000**. — [Copeps](https://copeps.fr/actualites/espace-pme-innovation/)
- **[Fact, sources conflict] i-Lab:** one source says up to EUR 600k, another says EUR 500k. **i-Nov:** EUR 200k to EUR 2.25M with co-financing, run as thematic waves (AI, health, energy, space, etc.). These amounts come from consultancy blogs, not bpifrance.fr. — [GrantBite](https://www.grantbite.com/en/blog/funding-for-startups-in-france-complete-guide); [modelesdebusinessplan](https://modelesdebusinessplan.com/blogs/infos/subventions-entreprise-innovante)
- **[Fact] France 2030 robotics strategy** (launched July 2023):
  - Has mobilised **EUR 250M+** of public aid across **100+** projects. — [entreprises.gouv.fr](https://www.entreprises.gouv.fr/fr/actualites/france-2030/france-2030-20-nouveaux-laureats-pour-l-appel-projets-offre-de-robots-et-machines-intelligentes-d); [info.gouv.fr](https://www.info.gouv.fr/index.php/actualite/france-2030-31-nouveaux-laureats-des-dernieres-releves-de-l-appel-a-projets-offre-de-robots-et-machines-intelligentes-d-excellence)

  - A CNRS/ANR robotics research programme received **EUR 30M** over 6.5 years. — same sources
  - **Pionniers de l'IA:** 23 first laureates got EUR 10M (10 Feb 2026). After a second round in June 2026 (28 more), the total is 51 projects. — [DGE](https://www.entreprises.gouv.fr/la-dge/actualites/appel-projets-pionniers-de-lia-les-23-premiers-laureats-connus); [CP 807](https://www.entreprises.gouv.fr/files/files/presse/2026/807%20-%20CP%20-%20France%202030%2028%20nouveaux%20laur%C3%A9ats%20de%20l%E2%80%99Appel%20%C3%A0%20projets%20%C2%AB%20Pionniers%20de%20l%E2%80%99IA%20%C2%BB.pdf)

Actual mobilisation (EUR 250M+) appears well below that headline. — [L'info durable](https://www.linfodurable.fr/france-2030-800-millions-deuros-pour-la-robotique-annonce-macron-29278)
  - A June 2025 VivaTech AI+robotics announcement is covered in [Atlanpole CP](https://www.atlanpole.fr/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/CP-IA-et-robotique-vf-VIVATECH-1-1.pdf). Frenchweb reported the State earmarking about EUR 1bn for startup industrial strategy at VivaTech 2025. — [FrenchWeb](https://www.frenchweb.fr/vivatech-2025-letat-muscle-sa-strategie-industrielle-pour-les-startups-francaises-avec-un-milliard-deuros-fleches/455396)

Sources conflict on the effective date: one says companies created from 2025, another says from 1 March 2025. — [Legifiscal](https://www.legifiscal.fr/actualites-fiscales/4186-jei-rehaussement-20-seuil-depenses-recherche.html); [GAC Group](https://group-gac.com/en/?p=300656)
- **[Fact] EIC Accelerator 2026:** grant **up to EUR 2.5M** for TRL 6-8 work (about 24 months), plus equity of **EUR 1-10M** (more possible under STEP Scale-Up). 2026 full-proposal cut-offs were 7 Jan, 4 Mar, 6 May, 8 Jul, 2 Sep and 4 Nov. Short proposals can be submitted any time, about 10 weeks before a cut-off. 2027 dates were not yet confirmed. — [Hezelburcht 2026 WP](https://www.hezelburcht.com/en/news/eic-launches-2026-work-programme-an-overview-of-the-eic-instruments-for-2026/); [Zabala](https://www.zabala.eu/news/eic-accelerator-2026-updates/); [Luxinnovation](https://luxinnovation.lu/news/horizon-europe’s-eic-in-2026-turn-breakthrough-ideas-into-market-success); [ABGI](https://www.abgi-uk.com/eic-accelerator-grant-funding-2026/)
- **[Fact]** The final 2025 EIC Accelerator cut-off drew strong interest. — [ERA Austria](https://www.era.gv.at/news-items/strong-interest-in-eic-accelerator-funding-in-final-2025-cut-off/)

### Inferences
- **[Inference]** A typical non-dilutive stack for year 1-2 of an AI+robotics startup:
  - BFT or BFT Émergence: EUR 30-90k
  - i-Lab: about EUR 500-600k, if a research link exists
  - i-Nov or France 2030 robotics calls: EUR 0.2-2M+
  - CIR on R&D salaries, plus CII at 20% on prototypes (max EUR 80k/yr)
  - JEI social-charge relief, if R&D is at least 20% of charges
  - EIC Accelerator once a pilot customer validates TRL 6+
- **[Inference]** The EIC Accelerator scores market traction. A signed first customer, even a sub-EUR 100k public "achat innovant" pilot, materially strengthens an EIC application. The two tracks should run in parallel.

### Gaps
- Official bpifrance.fr amounts for i-Lab, i-Nov and the Deeptech plan (e.g. "Prêt Deeptech", "Aide pour projet d'innovation") were not verified. The i-Lab figure conflicts between EUR 500k and EUR 600k.
- French Tech 2030 programme details (cohort, benefits), Next40/120 criteria and the EIC Pathfinder amounts were not researched in this session.
- Changes to CIR/CII/JEI under the 2026 finance law (LF 2026) were not checked. The CII ends 31 Dec 2027 unless extended.
- CIR headline rates (30% up to EUR 100M; higher for overseas departments) were not re-verified this session.

## 5. French/European AI and robotics companies and how they won early customers

### Takeaway
French robotics winners started with French retail or industrial anchor customers: Exotec with Cdiscount (and Decathlon), and Wandercraft with Renault, plus hospitals for its medical exoskeleton. They then exported. Acquisition by AI platforms (Pollen Robotics by Hugging Face) is an emerging exit pattern.

### Cited Findings
- **[Fact] Exotec** (founded 2015, Lille, by Romain Moulin and Renaud Heitz):
  - Cdiscount was among its first customers. It deployed Skypods near Bordeaux around 2017, with a reported 4x increase in order-processing speed.

## 6. Regulation: AI Act (and omnibus), GDPR/CNIL, CE marking, Machinery Regulation, PLD, CRA

### Takeaway
The 2026 Digital Omnibus deal postponed AI Act high-risk obligations: to **2 Dec 2027** for standalone (Annex III) systems and to **2 Aug 2028** for AI embedded in regulated products. The second date is the one that matters for robots under the Machinery Regulation. The Machinery Regulation 2023/1230 itself still applies from **20 Jan 2027**, so robotics hardware compliance is the nearer-term constraint.

### Cited Findings
- **[Fact]** EU co-legislators reached a provisional Digital Omnibus agreement on the AI Act on **7 May 2026**. High-risk obligations were postponed to **2 Dec 2027** for standalone systems and **2 Aug 2028** for product-embedded systems. Sandbox deadlines moved to 2 Aug 2027, and an Art. 50(2) labelling grace period runs to 2 Dec 2026. — [Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/eu-ai-act-omnibus-agreement-postponed-high-risk-deadlines-and-other-key-changes/) (seen via search summary; fetch blocked); [CASRAI](https://casrai.org/news/digital-omnibus-ai-act); [DLA Piper](https://knowledge.dlapiper.com/dlapiperknowledge/globalemploymentlatestdevelopments/2026/The-Digital-AI-Omnibus-Proposed-deferral-of-high-risk-AI-obligations-under-the-AI-Act)
- **[Fact, dates conflict]** Formal adoption: one source says the Council gave final approval on 29 June 2026, another says it has been in force since 27 July 2026. Verify against the Official Journal. — [SecurePrivacy](https://secureprivacy.ai/blog/eu-ai-act-digital-omnibus-the-new-high-risk-ai-deadlines-after-council-approval); [Software Improvement Group](https://www.softwareimprovementgroup.com/eu-ai-act-summary/)
- **[Fact]** The Commission's original Digital Omnibus proposal (Nov 2025) proposed a delay to 2027. — [BHRRC](https://www.bhrrc.org/es/latest-news/eu-commission-proposes-delay-to-ai-act-to-2027-amid-digital-omnibus-proposal/)
- **[Fact, official texts; dates from prior knowledge, not re-fetched this session]**
  - Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 applies from 20 Jan 2027 and replaces Directive 2006/42/EC. It adds requirements for AI/self-evolving safety functions and autonomous mobile machinery, plus third-party assessment for certain safety components with self-evolving behaviour. — [EUR-Lex 2023/1230](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1230/oj)
  - The revised Product Liability Directive (EU) 2024/2853 explicitly covers software and AI as "products". Member-state transposition is due by 9 Dec 2026. — [EUR-Lex 2024/2853](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2024/2853/oj)
  - The Cyber Resilience Act (EU) 2024/2847 brings vulnerability-reporting obligations from 11 Sep 2026 and full application from 11 Dec 2027. — [EUR-Lex 2024/2847](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/2847/oj)

### Inferences
- **[Inference]** For a first-customer pilot in 2026-27, French buyers' legal and HSE teams will ask about four things: (1) CE marking under the Machinery Directive now, with a path to the Machinery Regulation from Jan 2027; (2) GDPR/CNIL compliance if cameras capture workers or the public, including DPIA, works-council (CSE) consultation for workplace monitoring and data minimisation; (3) cybersecurity (CRA readiness, matching the corporate cyber brake noted by EY); (4) liability allocation under the new PLD. The AI Act high-risk regime is now a 2027-2028 concern, but buyers will still want an AI Act readiness statement.
- **[Inference]** CSE consultation is likely for any robot or AI system that changes working conditions or monitors employees. This adds weeks to French corporate deployments and should be planned into the timeline.

### Gaps
- No CNIL-specific 2025-2026 guidance on robotics or video analytics was retrieved.
- Whether the Omnibus changed any machinery-specific AI Act provisions was not confirmed from the primary text.
- No AI Act GPAI or AI-literacy dates were re-verified this session.

## 7. Cultural norms, payment terms (LME) and late-payment reality

### Takeaway
Under the LME rules, the legal maximum payment term is 60 days from invoice date, or 45 days end-of-month. Yet only **48%** of large French companies paid suppliers within the legal maximum in 2024. Late payment cost SMEs about **EUR 13bn** in trapped cash, according to the Banque de France ODP 2025 report. A startup should price, contract and finance accordingly.

### Cited Findings
- **[Fact]** In 2024, only **48%** of large companies paid suppliers before the legal maximum expired, down from 50% a year earlier. By contrast, **73%** of SMEs (excluding micro-enterprises) paid without delay. — [Banque de France press release ODP 2025](https://www.banque-france.fr/fr/communiques-de-presse/le-rapport-annuel-2025-de-lobservatoire-des-delais-de-paiement-des-resultats-contrastes-selon-les); [Rapport ODP 2025 PDF](https://www.banque-france.fr/system/files/2026-09/Rapport%20de%20l%27Observatoire%20des%20d%C3%A9lais%20de%20paiement%202025.pdf); [TPE Actu](https://tpeactu.fr/2026/09/26/retards-paiement-13-milliards-tpe-pme-grandes-entreprises/)
- **[Fact]** Late payments represent about **EUR 13bn** of cash that SMEs and micro-enterprises would otherwise have had, down from EUR 15bn in the previous report. Average supplier payment periods were about 49.2 days, versus 50 a year earlier. Use of the Médiateur des entreprises is rising. — [La Gazette France](https://www.lagazettefrance.fr/article/alerte-a-la-tresorerie-des-pme); [TPE Actu](https://tpeactu.fr/2026/09/26/retards-paiement-13-milliards-tpe-pme-grandes-entreprises/); [Banque de France](https://www.banque-france.fr/fr/actualites/rapport-de-lobservatoire-des-delais-de-paiement-2025)
- **[Fact]** Pacte PME large-account signatories report on-time payment above 80% for a growing number of members, with 61-90-day delays at 3%. — [Pacte PME Jan 2025](https://pactepme.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/CP-PactePME-Les-grands-comptes-francais-amplifient-leurs-efforts-pour-soutenir-la-resilience-des-PME-grace-a-des-pratiques-de-paiement-responsables-22-janvier-2025.pdf)
- **[Fact, legal rule; prior knowledge, not re-fetched]** Article L441-10 of the Code de commerce, from the LME of 2008, sets the maximum term at 60 days from invoice date, or 45 days end-of-month if agreed. The default without agreement is 30 days. A flat EUR 40 recovery indemnity and late-payment penalties apply. Public buyers must pay within 30 days (50 days for public hospitals). — [Légifrance L441-10](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000038414279)

### Inferences
- **[Inference]** For a first customer, a startup should do four things. Negotiate a 30-40% upfront milestone on hardware, since robots are capex the startup otherwise finances. Invoice promptly against signed acceptance (PV de réception). Prefer Pacte PME signatories or public buyers (30-day rule). Use Bpifrance invoice financing or factoring to bridge the gap.
- **[Inference / Opinion-grade]** Commonly reported French B2B norms are not sourced in this session: French-language materials and contracts, meetings with hierarchical sign-off (direction des achats plus the business unit plus the CSE for workplace change), long relationship-building, and slow August/December periods. Treat them as practitioner wisdom to verify.

### Gaps
- No 2023-2026 study on French B2B sales culture, decision-cycle length for robotics, or a "sell in French" requirement was retrieved.

## 9. Pricing and deal norms in EUR for POCs and first contracts

### Takeaway
No reliable published benchmarks were found for French corporate AI+robotics POC pricing. The two hard anchors are regulatory and public: the EUR 100k excl. VAT innovative-purchase threshold (EUR 60k for any supplies or services), and Reachy 2's price of about USD 70k.

### Cited Findings
- **[Fact]** Public innovative purchases can be direct-awarded below **EUR 100k excl. VAT**. Ordinary supplies and services can be direct-awarded below **EUR 60k excl. VAT** since Décret 2025-1386. — [collectivites-locales.gouv.fr](https://www.collectivites-locales.gouv.fr/index.php/animer-les-territoires/commande-publique/les-seuils-et-taux-applicables-aux-contrats-de-la-commande-publique); [AP-HP juridique](https://affairesjuridiques.aphp.fr/textes/decret-n-2025-1386-du-29-decembre-2025-modifiant-certains-seuils-relatifs-aux-marches-publics/)
- **[Fact]** Reachy 2 (Pollen/Hugging Face) is priced at about **USD 70,000**. — [Fortune](https://www.fortune.com/2025/04/14/ai-company-hugging-face-buys-humanoid-robot-company-pollen-robotics-reachy-2)
- **[Fact]** The CII covers 20% of up to EUR 400k/yr of innovation expenses such as prototypes and pilot installations, a maximum of EUR 80k credit. This effectively subsidises pilot-build costs for SMEs. — [Deloitte Avocats](https://blog.avocats.deloitte.fr/lf-et-lfss-pour-2025-panorama-des-differentes-mesures-prises-en-matiere-de-fiscalite-de-la-rd/)

### Inferences
- **[Inference]** Design the first paid pilot to land just under the relevant threshold. With public buyers, that means EUR 60k or EUR 100k excl. VAT. With corporates, it means the business unit's discretionary approval limit, which the startup should ask about directly. Structure it as hardware lease or Robot-as-a-Service plus a software subscription over 3-6 months, with a pre-agreed conversion price for deployment. Insist on paid (not free) POCs: a paid POC forces a budget owner, which addresses the "POC to deployment" brake.
- **[Inference]** Combine the pilot with CII (20%) and, where possible, a Bpifrance or France 2030 grant, so that the startup's net cost of a below-margin first pilot is partly subsidised.

The EY 2026 barometer and the Médiateur 2025 observatory report are the most likely sources but could not be read in full. Hosts blocked this session: ey.com, lafrenchtech.gouv.fr, gibsondunn.com.
