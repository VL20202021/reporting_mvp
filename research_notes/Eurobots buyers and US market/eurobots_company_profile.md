# Eurobots (Industrial Machinery Export Bilbao SL): Company Profile

Research date: November 2026. Method note: eurobots.net, robot-forum.com and directory.howtorobot.com could NOT be fetched directly from this research environment (network-blocked). Everything below comes from search-engine indexes and snippets of those pages and of registry sites. The snippets may be cached or old, so treat page-level details as "indexed, not live-verified". Anything that could not be checked against a second source is marked UNVERIFIED.

## 1. Website structure, product catalog and prices

### Takeaway
eurobots.net is a large multi-language catalog of used industrial robots, organized by brand and by application. It also sells spare parts, complete cells and DOBOT cobots. Most individual robot pages say "Ask for quote" instead of showing a price. Prices do appear on a few listings, on a "special offers" page, and on a sister welding-cell site (roboticweldingcells.com), where cells start at about €39.5k to €62.5k.

### Cited Findings

### Gaps

## 2. Refurbishment, warranty, inclusions, services, shipping and payment

### Takeaway
Eurobots says its robots are refurbished, inspected and fully tested, and carry a 6-month warranty. On the welding-cell site, the stated terms are EXW Bilbao (Incoterms 2020) and 30% deposit / 70% before shipment, paid by bank transfer, SEPA or letter of credit. Installation and commissioning are done through partners and are not included in the price.

### Cited Findings
- FAQ: "Eurobots recondition robots in the minimum details. Because of these various steps, we can provide a warranty to our robots for a period of 6 months." Its checklist includes "Teach pendant screen inspected", and it states robots are fully tested. — [FAQ](https://www.eurobots.net/faq-en.html)
- Robots are "reviewed beforehand by specialist technicians". The site also lists new and used spare parts, and integration and installation services (Mexico site). — [eurobots.com.mx](https://www.eurobots.com.mx/)
- Test videos of reconditioned units are published (e.g. KUKA KR15, ABB IRB 2400L, FANUC Arc Mate welding cells). — [Videos page](https://www.eurobots.net/video-used-robots-en.html)
- Listings are framed as robot plus controller (e.g. "with R-30iA controller", "control KRC1"). — [FANUC listing](https://www.eurobots.net/robot-r-2000ib-250f-used-fanuc-en.html); [KUKA listing](https://www.eurobots.net/KUKA-Shelf-robot-KR150K-1-control-KRC1-so7-in.html)
- Welding-cell terms:
  - default EXW Bilbao (Incoterms 2020)
  - shipping to the EU, UK, North America, Latin America, North Africa and the Middle East
  - payment 30% at order confirmation and 70% before shipment; other schedules for repeat customers or with a confirmed L/C
  - accepted methods: bank transfer, SEPA, L/C
  - 6-month limited warranty on robot, controller and welding power source
  - on-site installation and commissioning through the partner network, not included in the price
  — [Why Eurobots](https://roboticweldingcells.com/en/why-eurobots/); [Catalog](https://roboticweldingcells.com/en/catalog/)
- Know-how page: Eurobots "can deliver turn-key solutions worldwide", and it supplies robotic tooling through partners. — [Know-how](https://www.eurobots.net/know-how-en.html)

### Inferences
- The EXW and 30/70 terms are documented for welding cells. They are probably similar for single robots, but this is UNVERIFIED for eurobots.net robot sales.
- Because installation and commissioning go through partners, Eurobots acts mainly as a refurbisher and seller rather than a full-service integrator.

### Gaps
- I could not see whether a teach pendant, cables and software/licences are standard with every robot (the listing text was not retrievable).
- Warranty exclusions were not visible.
- I found no documented training or programming service with prices.

## 3. Business model, sister sites and international agents

### Takeaway
Eurobots is a stock-holding dealer and refurbisher, not a pure broker: it buys robots, refurbishes them in its own Zamudio warehouse and sells them. It runs many country and language domains and claims representatives in about 19–23 countries. I found no US representative.

## 5. Stated markets, countries and languages

### Takeaway
Eurobots presents itself as a worldwide exporter. It uses Spanish and English on its main sites, plus Italian, Ukrainian-market and Latin American country sites. Its stated focus regions are Europe, Latin America, the Middle East and India, and the welding-cell site explicitly adds North America.

### Cited Findings
- "Sells used robots worldwide"; the main warehouse is in Zamudio, "five minutes from the airport of Bilbao". — [About us](https://www.eurobots.net/about-us-en.html); [Where are we?](https://www.eurobots.net/whereweare-en.html)
- Language and market versions:
  - English pages use the `-en` / `-in` suffixes
  - Spanish pages use `-es`
  - Italian: eurobots.it and a YouTube channel in Italian
  - Latin American ccTLDs: MX, CO, AR, PE
  - Ukraine: .com.ua
  - India: .co.in
  — see the domain citations in section 3
- Shipping regions for welding cells: EU, UK, North America, Latin America, North Africa and the Middle East. — [Why Eurobots](https://roboticweldingcells.com/en/why-eurobots/)
- The representatives list (about 23 countries, US absent) is in section 3. — [Worldwide representatives](https://www.eurobots.net/worldwide_representatives-en.html)

### Inferences
- The US appears only as a shipping destination, with no localized site, representative or USD pricing found. This points to an underdeveloped US channel (inference).

### Gaps
- The full language-switcher list on eurobots.net (e.g. whether French, German, Portuguese or Russian versions exist) could not be confirmed, because the site could not be fetched live.
