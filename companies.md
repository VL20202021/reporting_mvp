## Notes and assumptions

- **Archetype assignment** follows the company's main revenue engine. Many companies also have a `secondary_archetype`, for example MercadoLibre (marketplace → fintech), Apple (hardware → services), Reddit (ads → data licensing), Spotify (freemium → subscription).
- **Non-US** counts HQ or main operating market. Accenture (Dublin-domiciled), Coupang (US-listed, Korea-operated) and Globant (Luxembourg-domiciled, Argentine origin) are counted as non-US. Even without these three there are 34 non-US companies.
- **Status as of today:** Figma (IPO Jul 2025), Chime (Jun 2025), Klarna (Sep 2025), CoreWeave (Mar 2025) and Swiggy (Nov 2024) are counted as public. Iconix, Evernote and Grammarly are private because they were acquired or taken private.
- **Data-risk companies**, the most likely swaps if sourcing is thin: Faire, Gymshark, SKIMS, Whoop, Notion, Glossier, Mistral AI. Each has a same-archetype backup ready, e.g. Whatnot, Liquid Death, Ramp, Miro.
